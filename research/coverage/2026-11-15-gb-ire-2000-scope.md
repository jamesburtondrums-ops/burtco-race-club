# GB and Ireland — last 2,000 completed races research scope

Scope confirmed 2026-11-15. Research-only; no alteration to frozen A/B1, selections or paper ledger.

## Inclusion
- Last 2,000 **distinct completed** races in Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales) and Ireland (Northern Ireland and Republic of Ireland), sorted by verified actual off time descending, then unique race ID to break ties.
- Flat turf, all-weather, hurdles, chases and National Hunt flat. Exclude overseas races, abandoned races and duplicate provider records. Non-runners are preserved separately from starters.
- Preserve each race's actual course, discipline, class/race type, distance, going, draw where relevant, official rating, actual carried weight and claim, headgear, rider, trainer, full result, race comments and stewards incidents where available.
- Every runner: stable horse ID, three own previous individual results plus older relevant performances, source URL, capture timestamp, rights status, as-of/validity date and evidence uncertainty. No invented BHA achieved figures, sectionals or pace styles.

## Current status and next execution steps
- **Target**: 2,000 distinct races, not 2,000 runners. Runner total will be whatever the verified full fields establish.
- Current GitHub branch contains historical reviews and coverage references, but no demonstrated rights-cleared 2,000-race archive. Target count is not completed.
- Build a deduplicated manifest of existing race IDs, then enumerate completed GB/Ireland races backward from 2026-11-15 with source and observed-at timestamps. Lock a reproducible race cohort before extensive backfill.
- Obtain lawful/licensed bulk result and form access for large-scale archival reuse; do not commit copyrighted raw third-party text or bypass access controls. Until rights are confirmed, store restricted source metadata and short original findings only.
- Import in small verified batches, with checkpoints showing target races, discovered IDs, full-field reconciled races, verified last-three histories, permissioned runner rows, and missing data. Quarantine conflicts.
- Compare BHA OR against verified achieved BHA performance figures with their publication/revision times; distinguish provider RPR and other ratings. Sectionals/par only with published, appropriately comparable data.
- Challenger C is shadow only: pre-off frozen prospective cards and as-at market quotes are required for walk-forward, ranking coverage and calibration claims. Post-race backfill is not pre-race prediction.

## Research gates
- Preserve source IDs, capture times and exact horse/race identities. No automatic bonuses for headgear or unusual jockey bookings; use matched comparable opportunities, denominators, claims, availability and market baseline, with shrinkage and chronological holdouts.
- No named published selection from retrospective-only research; never overwrite frozen grades.
