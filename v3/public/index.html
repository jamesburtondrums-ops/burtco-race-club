How to read this assessment

Every declared horse receives a probability, and the full field adds up to 100%. The fixed experimental ensemble uses age, weight, draw, handicap position, fitness, prior form, course/trip/going evidence and connections. Market forecasts do not influence those ability probabilities.

Race-day research now compares winning handicap marks within discipline and surface, condition records with sample sizes, current pre-race RPR/TS across the field and seasonal returns to winning conditions. These new diagnostics are unvalidated research evidence and do not receive invented probability bonuses.

Recent race comments provide a separate, explicitly inferred pace assessment. Pedigree, published form lines and available achieved figures are shown for review. Missing evidence remains visible.

Country-suffix variants are reconciled using the declared horse's origin. Ambiguous person names retain their source spelling. These data corrections have not been independently validated as an improvement in prediction accuracy.

Historical v3 testing did not establish an improvement sufficient for promotion. The production model is retained; these are separately recorded experimental forecasts.

Price and place terms

Source "probable SP" figures are market forecasts, not live bookmaker offers. No each-way place terms are verified, so this page does not assume extra places or present forecast prices as available bets. The shortlist requires at least evens and a fresh price/going check.
