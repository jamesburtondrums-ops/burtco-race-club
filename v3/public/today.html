Evidence and immutable records

The review uses declared fields from IrishRacing, current factual ratings and withdrawals from Racing Post, and previous numerical results and race-comment classifications from the historical archive. Website tipping verdicts and current prices do not enter the ability model. RPR on today's card is a published assessment; prior achieved RPR is labelled separately.

Trainer 14-day figures reflect matched archive coverage through 22 October. Missing coverage is shown as unknown. Jumping-card last-run dates can omit later Flat starts. Pace and comment flags are inferred from earlier race comments and may be imperfect. Heavy and soft are separate categories in the numerical diagnostics.

Some Irish bumpers are labelled Flat in the archive. Newcomers, overseas form gaps and code changes receive limited evidence status; they are not assumed to have poor ability. Unconfirmed prior archive entries do not count as manual evidence of completed starts.
