## Mandatory pre-promotion research standard

Before a candidate is upgraded to a fresh WIN or each-way recommendation, record individually: (a) three latest comparable races, going/distance/class and beaten margins, (b) today's handicap mark versus actual previous winning/placing marks plus jockey claim, (c) recent trainer-race-type and jockey/horse partnership with denominator, (d) any stable, headgear or discipline change and its historical evidence, (e) course draw side, likely early pace and rivals that can improve, (f) 2 timestamped primary sources with discrepancies, (g) exact live each-way places and price separately from winning-confidence assessment. Explicitly write UNKNOWN if evidence cannot be confirmed. Record final research decision before off, never overwrite original snapshot or create stakes.
