## TRIPLE LOCK 1 — Whole-field evidence (before ANY horse is nominated)

Every actual declared runner must have a separately recorded evidence row, even at 50/1 or 200/1. Verify racing identity and starter status. Check for **each runner**:

1. Last three runs, plus any older *directly relevant* race; date/course/code/class/conditions/opponents/rating/weight/claim, lengths, finishing position and actual running comments (and replay if accessible). For a debutant, all available trials/pedigree and a clearly recorded lack of racecourse evidence.
2. Today's trip, track shape/stiffness, going and surface; past *achieved* performance on similar terms; handicap mark versus mark on the comparable run; penalties, class rise/drop, and physical weight after verified rider allowances. Don't double count claims.
3. Early/late pace options, draw where material, field pace competition, jumping/settling/start reliability and realistic stamina test.
4. Exactly which headgear is worn today, what was worn previously, whether it is first time/reapplied/removed, and any **previous evidence** that this horse responds to the same equipment. No unconditional headgear-positive bonus.
5. Jockey: actual booking and weight claim, previous rider, horse/rider experience, course/code track record, recent rides and reasons for an apparently unusual appointment if independently verifiable.
6. Trainer: recent and longer-form yard performance; course record by relevant discipline, trainer record by **race type** (e.g. 2yo debut/novice/handicap/chase) and trip/going when sample permits; previous target patterns; stable switch. No fabricated percentages.
7. Source links and exact pre-race capture time for every factual item; mark missing values **unknown**, never zero, weak or negative without justification.

**Pass:** every runner entered and every evidence domain checked as verified or explicitly unknown with a source/reason. **Fail:** missing runner, missing evidence provenance, non-runner uncertainty, or silent unknown. A race cannot be called triple-locked if Lock 1 fails.

## TRIPLE LOCK 2 — Adversarial rival challenge

Before selecting, write the strongest honest case for every runner, even when it is ultimately rejected. For the three strongest plausible alternatives (or all rivals in a four-runner field), write:

- Where could the rival exceed its **already demonstrated** performance? Mark class drop, lighter terms, race code switch, step up/down in trip, course/going return, pace change, suitable return from break, equipment changes, trainer transfer, jockey partnership, pedigree (for lightly raced horses), and a credible unexposed ceiling.
- Compare today's rival with the first choice **head-to-head** on achieved performance, not win counts, market rank or recent headline finishing position.
- Explain *specifically* why our leader might get beaten. If no persuasive rebuttal to one serious rival, call the contest Open/Tentative, not Clear.
- Stress-test **at least three** pace scenarios: expected, stronger early pace, and slower/positionally disadvantaged. Over jumps, explicitly consider jumping mistakes/stamina pressure.
- Repeat original Rules v2 one-grade sensitivity; challenge selected horse down one grade and each rival up one grade one at a time; test unknown components over 0–5 endpoints. If the winner changes in any plausible perturbation, flag selection as sensitive.

**Important:** an unexposed runner's *unknown* ability is not poor ability. Its optimistic ceiling can prevent a high-confidence assessment without being evidence it should automatically replace the first pick.

### Special research hypothesis: small yard booking a high-performing jockey

This is a **lead for investigation**, not proof the stable is expecting a win. Record: trainer's usual riders at this course/code, previous booking, the newly booked rider's record **adjusted for the quality and price of mounts**, travel/availability, weight/claim, existing professional relationships, and trainer–rider pair data over relevant periods. Compare with similar rides for this yard, not raw elite-jockey strike rate alone.

- **Speculative flag:** unusual jockey with unverified motivation, or fewer than 20 comparable prior rides. It *must* appear in the runner/rival audit but provides **no automatic numeric score boost**.
- **Research signal:** at least 20 comparable rides *plus* a sustained market-adjusted association after provisional shrinkage, and no obvious availability/claim explanation. The included script reports both raw actual/expected (A/E) and a regularised A/E using a fixed illustrative 30-ride prior. These thresholds are research conventions, not learned winning rules.
- **Corroborated capability:** only prior evidence showing that the specific combination improves execution for this type of horse may justify a documented adjustment to E, F or P grades. Avoid double counting the rider's claim or interpreting an anecdotal booking as stable intent.
- **Calibration:** once enough forward-collected data exists, compare unusual bookings to matched **non-unusual** bookings controlling for starting-price implied chance, race code, class and trainer volume; compare incremental top-3 winner coverage and SP ROI. If the edge disappears, discard the flag's predictive use.

**Why measure versus the market?** High-profile jockey/trainer teams often win frequently but attract shorter prices. A retrospective dataset of 1.8m UK/Ireland runners reported that backing established pairings indiscriminately lost at SP; that is a reason to test unusual bookings, not to assume an edge. Source: https://theoverlay.co.uk/jockey-trainer-combinations-horse-racing.html (third-party retrospective analysis; methodological assumptions may differ).

## TRIPLE LOCK 3 — Live readiness, re-ranking, immutable freeze

For each race: recheck official going/course inspection; non-runners; jockey changes/claims/weight; headgear; stable declarations; changed trip or track conditions; recorded market quotes and substantial late moves **as a diagnostic not a selection driver**. Re-run Lock 1/2 if a material input changes. Record named source, UTC capture time, selection version, score, runner ranks, rival, unknowns and reasons; freeze the record **before the scheduled off**. Late replacements retain the previous forecast, with a new time-stamped revision, never overwrite it. Nothing should be retrospectively inserted from the result.

**Pass:** all confirmations and freeze present. **Hold:** any check missing or unstable; no *verified* selection, and no high-confidence label.

## Two different scores, explicitly not probabilities

### 1. Performance Evidence Index — /100

Retain the Rules v2 arithmetic grading priorities when estimating demonstrated ability from **reassessed** pre-race evidence:

`8 × Ability + 5 × Conditions + 3 × Execution + 2 × Readiness + 2 × Pace`, each component 0–5. Unknown = U, provisionally 2.5 **for ordering only**, with 0–5 low/high uncertainty bounds shown. Headgear, bookings and trainer patterns affect these grades **only when supported by actual evidence**; otherwise they create audit flags and possible selection uncertainty. No extra hidden bonus for big names or sudden odds changes.

### 2. Audit Conviction Index — /100

| Audit domain | Weight | What earns a high mark |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence integrity | 20 | All entrants + comparable, sourced records and no critical missing values |
| Comparative ability edge | 20 | Demonstrated advantage after mark/class/terms and opposition comparisons |
| Strongest-rival resilience | 25 | Leading choice survives the best credible opposition case and sensitivity challenges |
| Pace/conditions resilience | 15 | Different plausible run shapes and going assumptions don't reverse the choice |
| Trainer/jockey/equipment evidence | 10 | Booking and headgear are verified and assessed rather than merely asserted |
| Late declarations stability | 10 | Race terms and named participants rechecked near the freeze |

Grade each of the six domains 0–5 and multiply by its weight/5. **This is an unvalidated auditable decision-support index, NOT a 70/80/90% win chance.** It must be stored alongside the written evidence, and is blank until all component checks are completed. A selected runner with any unknown core grade has conviction capped at 65; a sensitivity failure caps at 70; unresolved material rival at 60. These are prospective conservative reporting gates, not data-fitted probabilities. A value of 85 is not a forecast 85% win chance.

- **80–100 with all three locks passing and no sensitivity failures:** Supported research preference (NOT 80% likely winner).
- **Under 80, or any sensitivity failure:** Tentative research preference, named best rival and failure reason shown.
- **Any missing gate:** Hold; do not label the race thoroughly checked. The system may show an explicit incomplete provisional ranking only.

Only if a separately trained and *held-out calibrated* win-probability model exists will a probability and fair odds be published. Compare forecast bins with observed wins and Brier/log scores on untouched future races; sample minimum 100 prospective races for an initial quality checkpoint, with far more required to validate uncommon booking signals.
